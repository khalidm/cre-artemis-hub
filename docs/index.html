The Research Challenge & Objectives

Overcoming the fragmented nature of early-onset bowel cancer datasets by integrating multi-layered genomic and clinical data to uncover underlying biological mechanisms and novel therapeutic targets.

Advanced AI & Machine Learning

Enabling state-of-the-art ML-driven data integration to generate coherent, multi-layered datasets that are impossible to achieve through isolated analyses.

Translational Research

Promoting new discoveries beyond the scope of individual projects by providing an enduring, unified resource available to the global research community.

Capacity Building

Providing high-quality data to rigorously support EMCR and PhD research outputs, underpinned by experienced computational biologists and statisticians.

The ARTEMIS Platform Workflow

Interact with our GUARDIANS-backed infrastructure through a secure, streamlined three-step pipeline designed for rigorous analysis.

1. Explore & Build

Filter harmonised variables across genomic, clinical, and biospecimen records to precisely fit specific research questions.

2. Apply for Access

Submit requests managed via robust Data Access Management tools, ensuring strict compliance with participant privacy.

3. Analyse Securely

Conduct advanced computational analysis within scalable Trusted Research Environments (TREs), supported by the CI & AI team.

This architecture and centralized framework aligns strictly with the specifications detailed in the Project data repository and analysis design report.
